Business Profile

More than coaching.

A focused learning environment combining expert teaching, structured practice and personalised academic support.

Don’t just prepare for the exam. Prepare to perform.

CETEdge is an MHT-CET coaching centre built for Maharashtra students aiming at engineering and pharmacy entrance pathways. Programmes cover Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics for PCM students, and Physics, Chemistry and Biology for PCB students.

Teaching is concept-based: every chapter starts with the why before the how, so students can solve unfamiliar questions instead of memorising patterns.

Regular practice tests, previous-year paper analysis and dedicated doubt-solving sessions turn understanding into speed and accuracy. Study material and revision resources keep preparation organised from first chapter to final week.

Each student receives personalised guidance and performance tracking, with online and offline options so preparation fits around school and board exams.
